Research that puts human thinking first.

My research program brings together human-centered AI, scientific reasoning, self-regulated learning, and emerging technologies in science and STEM education.

Core research areas
01 — Human–AI learning

Human-Centered AI & Feedback Design

My work examines direct, reflective, and hybrid generative-AI feedback and the conditions in which technological support improves scientific reasoning rather than merely improving an AI-assisted product. A central concern is whether learners maintain evaluative judgement and argument quality when support is removed.

02 — Reasoning and agency

Scientific Reasoning & Self-Regulated Learning

This strand focuses on evidence-based scientific argumentation, inquiry, feedback uptake, revision processes, and self-regulated learning. Experimental approaches evaluate learning outcomes and transfer across contexts, including delayed assessments.

03 — STEM and educational design

Emerging Technologies in STEM Education

Research integrates virtual and augmented reality, educational robotics, intelligent tutoring, and gamification. I investigate both instructional effectiveness and educators' technology adoption using theory-driven quantitative models.
