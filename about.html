Hüseyin Ateş, PhD — Associate Professor of Science Education, Kırşehir Ahi Evran University, Türkiye.

Academic biography

Teaching, research & academic service.

My research focuses on human-centered artificial intelligence, scientific reasoning, and self-regulated learning. I investigate educational feedback, teachers' technology adoption, and research-informed instructional change. I have coordinated a published four-university experiment and supervise master's and doctoral research.
