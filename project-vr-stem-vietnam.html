VR-Supported STEM Learning

Experimental Investigation of the Effects of VR-Supported STEM Instruction on Middle School Students' Academic Achievement, STEM Attitudes, and Technology Acceptance in Türkiye, and Evaluation of Its Adaptability to the Vietnamese Educational Context

Project period2026–2028
Funding bodyKırşehir Ahi Evran University · BAP
Principal investigatorHüseyin Ateş
Research overview

Research rationale.

This funded university research project studies VR-supported STEM instruction among middle school students in Türkiye. It also includes an evaluation of how the instructional approach may be adapted to the Vietnamese educational context.
