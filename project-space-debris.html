Space-Debris Problem-Solving

A Classroom Pilot Comparison of Two Pedagogical Scaffolding Approaches in Space-Debris Problem-Solving Instruction

Project period2026–2027
Funding bodyTÜBİTAK · 1002-A Short-Term Support Module
Principal investigatorHüseyin Ateş
Research overview

Research rationale.

This classroom pilot compares two approaches to pedagogical scaffolding in instruction centred on solving space-debris problems. The project focuses on how instructional support is structured during science problem-solving tasks.
