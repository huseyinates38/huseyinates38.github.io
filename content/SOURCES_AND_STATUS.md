## Teaching resources

The three toolkits, bilingual Explorer scenario, plant CSV and variable-dictionary template are original site-created educational prototypes, prepared with AI-assisted drafting. They are neither participant data nor reproduced study instruments. The fictional plant comparison intentionally varies both water and light, illustrating why the effect of light cannot be isolated from the supplied comparison.

The website does not claim that these prototypes have been reviewed in a trial, validated psychometrically or adopted by schools. No open licence is assigned. Author approval is needed for a subsequent licensing decision.
